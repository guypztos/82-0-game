Can your team go 82-0?

Eight rounds. Each round rolls a random real NBA team from any season since 1979-80, and you draft one player from that roster: that season's version of him. Pick five starters and three bench players, then watch your team play 82 games against real teams from history.

You get two rerolls for the whole draft. Spend them wisely.

Use a specific seed

The same seed with the same picks always plays the same season.
